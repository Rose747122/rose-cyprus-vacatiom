The Arkin Iskele hotel

Our family vacation in Cyprus was truly unforgettable. We stayed at The Arkın İskele, a luxurious five-star hotel on the coast of North Cyprus. The hotel has a beautiful private beach, sparkling swimming pools and comfortable rooms with stunning sea views. Its restaurants serve delicious dishes from around the world, so every meal felt like a small adventure. Families will love the water park with its exciting slides, and younger guests can spend their days at the special kids’ club. For a quieter moment, the hotel also offers a relaxing spa, an indoor pool and a well-equipped fitness center .
